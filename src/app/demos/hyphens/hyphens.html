Hyphens

Use hyphens to control how words are broken across lines. manual only breaks at a soft hyphen (&shy;), while auto lets the browser hyphenate based on the lang attribute.

@for (mode of modes; track mode.id) { } 
<p lang="en">
  An extra&shy;ordinary sesqui&shy;pedalian word.
</p>

p {
  hyphens: {{ selectedMode().id }};
}

With soft hyphens (&shy;)

The tele­communi­cations depart­ment announced an extra­ordinary reorgan­ization of its infra­structure, describing the counter­productive documen­tation as incompre­hensible and thoroughly unimagi­native.

Without soft hyphens

The telecommunications department announced an extraordinary reorganization of its infrastructure, describing the counterproductive documentation as incomprehensible and thoroughly unimaginative.

  • A soft hyphen (&shy;) is invisible until the browser needs to break the word at that exact position.
  • hyphens: auto requires a lang attribute so the browser knows which hyphenation dictionary to use.
  • Both columns are intentionally narrow so that lines are short enough to force hyphenation, even on wide screens.
